March Madness Predictor

Machine learning

Predicting how far a team advances.

An ordinal neural network uses engineered efficiency metrics to estimate cumulative round progression probabilities, from the round of 64 to champion. A mean absolute error of 0.72 rounds puts predictions typically within one round of the actual finish; tournament randomness and class imbalance remain limitations.
